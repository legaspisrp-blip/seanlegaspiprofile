## 📸 Suggested screenshots to attach (carousel posts get 3× more engagement)

LinkedIn lets you upload up to 9 images per post. Here's the recommended set, in order:

1. **Hero shot** — Open your home page. Scroll to the very top. Screenshot the whole "Building structure where there is chaos." headline with the metric strip below it.
2. **The work list** — Go to Work page. Screenshot the full list of 11 roles with their categories.
3. **A single case study** — Click into MNT Scientific or Black Vault Media. Screenshot the role title + the three outcome metrics (the big serif numbers).
4. **The journal grid** — Go to Journal. Screenshot the featured 3-card row at the top.
5. **An article in reader view** — Open any article. Screenshot the cover image + first paragraph.
6. **The tools grid** — On Home or About, scroll to "Tools & Stack." Screenshot the chips with logos.
7. **About page** — Screenshot the portrait + monogram block on the left, with your bio on the right.
8. **Tweaks panel** — Open the Tweaks panel (toolbar toggle). Show the palette / font / density controls.
9. **Dark mode** — Toggle dark mode, then screenshot the hero again. Two-of-the-same-screen comparisons get attention.

**Tip:** crop screenshots to 1200×627px (LinkedIn's preferred ratio) so they don't get awkwardly cropped in the feed.

## 💡 Tips for the launch

1. **Post at 9–10 AM Manila time on a Tuesday or Wednesday.** Best LinkedIn engagement window.
2. **Reply to every comment in the first 60 minutes.** Each reply re-boosts the post to more feeds.
3. **Don't link in the main post body.** LinkedIn deprioritizes posts with external links. Put `[your URL]` *in the first comment* instead, or write *"link in comments."*
4. **Tag 2–3 past clients or operators you respect** so their networks see it.
5. **Re-share weekly for the first 4 weeks** with different angles: "here's what I do," "case study X," "journal post Y," "tools I run on."
